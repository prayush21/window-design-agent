If you can only send three things

Not every field will exist in your system. These three determine whether a recommendation is possible at all; everything else improves its quality.

  1. A flat swatch image per colorway, named by variant_id The single most valuable item in this request.
  2. Opacity class Sheer, light-filtering, room-darkening, or blackout.
  3. Minimum and maximum width and height Whether the product physically fits the window.

File format

CSV or XLSX, UTF-8, first row as headers, using the exact column names below. If you already export from a PIM in a different shape, send that instead along with a column glossary — we will map it. Leave a field blank rather than guessing: a blank is information, a guess is not.

Swatch images

A flat swatch is how we match a product's color to a customer's room. A photograph of the product hanging in a styled room cannot do this — the room's paint, lighting, and white balance all shift the apparent color, and we end up matching the wrong thing.

  • Fill the frame with the material: no room, no window, no props
  • 1000 × 1000 px minimum, square
  • Neutral background — white or 18% gray, where any is visible
  • Even, neutral lighting around 5000–6500 K, with no colored bounce and no strong shadow
  • sRGB, JPEG at quality 90 or above, or PNG
  • No color grading or retouching. An unedited accurate photo beats a beautiful inaccurate one
  • Show the real weave and texture at full resolution

Product and lifestyle images

Send these as well if you have them. They're genuinely useful for showing drape, fold, hang, and scale, and for generating room previews — they simply can't substitute for a flat swatch.

Color accuracy

Color is the thing we most need to get right, and the thing most easily lost in photography. Any one of these solves it, in order of preference.

  • A measured color_hex, from a spectrophotometer or your design source files
  • A color reference card — X-Rite ColorChecker or similar — in frame on a few shots, so we can calibrate the rest
  • Swatch photographs shot to the lighting specification above

If it's easier to send physical swatch cards, we can measure them ourselves. Let us know and we'll arrange collection.

What we're not asking for

We don't need style tags, room recommendations, "best for" or "avoid for" guidance, or pairing suggestions. Those are aesthetic judgments we make ourselves, and a guess in those fields is worse for us than a blank.

Please spend the time on accurate physical specifications instead — dimensions, opacity, material, and color.

Updates

  • Keep product_id and variant_id stable permanently. If a color is reformulated, issue a new ID rather than changing what an existing one refers to.
  • Send discontinued items with status = discontinued rather than deleting the rows, so we can retire them cleanly instead of discovering a gap.
  • A full refresh on any regular cadence works. Tell us what's realistic on your side.
If a required field is genuinely unavailable, tell us which one and we'll work out a fallback rather than have you invent a value.
